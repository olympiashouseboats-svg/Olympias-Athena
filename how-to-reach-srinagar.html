How to Reach Srinagar

Flights, Trains & Road Routes Compared — 2026

Getting to Srinagar: Your Options

Srinagar sits in a Himalayan valley, which shapes every option for getting there — flying is fast and straightforward, while road and rail involve navigating mountain terrain. Here's an honest comparison of each route so you can pick based on your budget, timeline, and whether the journey itself is part of the trip for you.

Quick Answer

Flying is fastest and most practical for most travelers — Srinagar Airport has regular connections from Delhi (1.5 hours), Mumbai, and other major cities, with flights from ₹4,000-12,000 one-way. By train, long-distance services reach Jammu, requiring a further 7-8 hour road journey to Srinagar. By road, NH44 from Jammu is a well-traveled, scenic but long route, best for those wanting a road trip rather than the fastest option.

By Air

Srinagar Airport (SXR) has direct connections from Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Kolkata, and several other major Indian cities, with multiple daily flights during peak season. Delhi-Srinagar is the most common route, taking around 1.5 hours. Book 3-4 weeks ahead for the best fares, especially for summer travel (June-August) when demand peaks. Prices roughly double during the busiest weeks compared to shoulder-season fares.

By Train

Long-distance trains from Delhi, Mumbai, and other major cities connect to Jammu Tawi railway station, the gateway to the valley. From Jammu, it's approximately 270 km and 7-8 hours by road via NH44 to reach Srinagar. Within the valley itself, a separate rail line connects Banihal, Srinagar, and Baramulla — useful for local travel but not (currently) a through-route from the rest of India. Valley rail connectivity has been expanding in recent years, so it's worth checking the latest Indian Railways updates before planning around this option.

By Road

The Jammu-Srinagar Highway (NH44) is the main road route, a well-paved and regularly used highway through the Pir Panjal range via the Jawahar Tunnel. It's scenic and increasingly used for road trips, but it's a long journey — roughly 270 km taking 7-8 hours from Jammu alone, and considerably longer from Delhi (20+ hours total). Monsoon season (July-August) occasionally brings landslide-related closures, so build in buffer time if self-driving during that period.

From Srinagar Airport to Dal Lake

Whichever route you take, the final leg is the same: Srinagar Airport is approximately 14-16 km from Boulevard Road on Dal Lake, about a 30-40 minute taxi ride. Prepaid taxi counters are available at the airport. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fastest way to reach Srinagar?

Flying is by far the fastest option — direct flights from Delhi take about 1.5 hours, compared to 20+ hours by road or a combination of train to Jammu plus a road journey.

Can I take a train directly to Srinagar?

The Kashmir Valley rail line (Banihal-Srinagar-Baramulla) operates within the valley itself, but direct long-distance trains from major Indian cities currently terminate at Jammu, requiring a road connection (roughly 270 km, 7-8 hours) onward to Srinagar. Check current Indian Railways updates, as valley rail connectivity has been expanding.

How much does a flight to Srinagar cost?

Flights from Delhi typically range ₹4,000-12,000 one-way depending on season and how far in advance you book, with prices rising significantly during peak summer months (May-July).

Is it safe to drive to Srinagar?

The Jammu-Srinagar National Highway (NH44) is a well-traveled, paved route used regularly by tourists and commercial traffic, though it can experience landslide-related closures during monsoon season (July-August) — check road conditions before a self-drive trip.

How do I get from Srinagar Airport to Dal Lake?

Srinagar Airport is approximately 14-16 km from Boulevard Road on Dal Lake, roughly a 30-40 minute taxi ride. Most houseboat owners can arrange airport pickup with advance notice.
