Gulmarg: The Meadow of Flowers

Gulmarg — literally "Meadow of Flowers" — is Kashmir's most famous hill station, home to one of the world's highest cable cars and India's premier ski destination. At 2,650 meters elevation, ringed by the Pir Panjal range, Gulmarg offers something almost no other Indian destination can: genuine, reliable Alpine skiing in winter and lush, flower-covered meadows in summer, all reachable in under two hours from Dal Lake.

Quick Answer

Gulmarg is 52 km (1.5-2 hours) from Srinagar and accessible year-round, unlike Sonamarg or Zojila Pass. The main draw is the Gulmarg Gondola — one of the world's highest cable cars, rising to Apharwat Peak at 3,979m — used for skiing in winter (December-March) and panoramic sightseeing year-round. A combined Gondola ticket costs roughly ₹1,700-1,900. Most visitors do Gulmarg as a day trip from Srinagar, basing themselves at a Dal Lake houseboat.

Top Attractions in Gulmarg

Gulmarg Gondola (Cable Car)

One of the highest cable cars in the world, operating in two phases: Gulmarg to Kongdoori (Phase 1) and Kongdoori to Apharwat Peak (Phase 2) at 3,979 meters. The views over the Pir Panjal range and, on clear days, toward Nanga Parbat in Pakistan-administered Kashmir, are extraordinary. Best visited early morning to avoid queues and clouds.

Apharwat Peak

The top station of the Gondola's second phase, offering snow year-round at the summit and Kashmir's best skiing terrain in winter. Even non-skiers come up for snow play, sledging, and photography against Himalayan backdrops.

Gulmarg Golf Course

One of the highest golf courses in the world (2,650m), open in summer months when the meadow turns green. A striking, if niche, attraction for golf enthusiasts wanting a genuinely unusual round.

Ningle Nallah

A scenic stream near Gulmarg, popular for short walks and picnics in the summer months when the surrounding meadows are at their greenest.

St. Mary's Church

A charming 19th-century colonial-era church, a quiet architectural contrast to Gulmarg's alpine scenery and a popular quick stop for visitors.

Activities by Season

Winter (December-March): Skiing and snowboarding (equipment rental and instructors widely available), sledging, snow tubing, and heli-skiing for advanced skiers at Apharwat's off-piste terrain.

Summer (April-June): Golf at the high-altitude course, meadow walks, horse riding, and photography of wildflower-covered slopes.

Monsoon (July-August): Cooler than the plains and generally green, though occasional cloud cover can affect Gondola visibility — still a pleasant, less-crowded time to visit.

Best Time to Visit Gulmarg

Unlike Sonamarg or Zojila Pass, Gulmarg is accessible year-round since it doesn't sit on a route that closes for winter. December-March is peak ski season and the most popular time to visit; April-June offers green meadows and golf without ski crowds. Frequently Asked Questions About Gulmarg

How far is Gulmarg from Srinagar?

Gulmarg is approximately 52 km from Srinagar, roughly a 1.5-2 hour drive. A round-trip taxi typically costs ₹2,000-3,000.

How much does the Gulmarg Gondola cost?

The Gondola operates in two stages: Phase 1 (Gulmarg to Kongdoori) costs approximately ₹740-800 round trip, and Phase 2 (Kongdoori to Apharwat Peak) costs an additional ₹980-1,050. A combined Phase 1+2 ticket runs roughly ₹1,700-1,900 per person, though prices change periodically.

What is the best time to visit Gulmarg?

December to March is best for snow and skiing, with January-February offering the deepest snow. April to June is best for green meadows, golf, and pleasant weather without the ski crowds. Gulmarg is accessible year-round, unlike Sonamarg or Zojila Pass.

Can Gulmarg be visited as a day trip from Srinagar?

Yes, Gulmarg is commonly visited as a day trip from Srinagar or Dal Lake houseboats, leaving early morning and returning by evening, though an overnight stay is worthwhile in winter for a full ski day without rushing.

Do I need to book Gondola tickets in advance?

During peak winter season (December-February) and major holidays, Gondola tickets can sell out or involve long queues, so booking online in advance or arriving early is strongly recommended.
