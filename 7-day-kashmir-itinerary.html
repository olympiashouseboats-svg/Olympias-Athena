7-Day Kashmir Itinerary

Srinagar Houseboat Base + Gulmarg, Pahalgam & Sonamarg — 2026

The Classic First-Timer's Kashmir Week

Seven days is the sweet spot for a first Kashmir trip — enough time to properly experience Dal Lake and Srinagar without rushing, plus a full day trip each to Gulmarg, Pahalgam, and Sonamarg, Kashmir's three signature hill stations. This itinerary is built around basing yourself at one Dal Lake houseboat for the whole week, which cuts down on packing/unpacking and lets your host help coordinate each day's taxi.

Quick Answer

A well-paced 7-day Kashmir trip: Days 1-2 settle into a Dal Lake houseboat and explore Srinagar's Mughal gardens; Day 3 Gulmarg; Day 4 Pahalgam; Day 5 Sonamarg; Day 6 a deeper Dal Lake day (floating market, floating gardens, shopping); Day 7 departure. Budget roughly ₹35,000-70,000 per person excluding flights, depending on houseboat tier and season.

Day-by-Day Plan

Day 1: Arrival & Houseboat Check-In

Fly or drive into Srinagar, transfer to Boulevard Road, and take your first Shikara ride out to your houseboat. Spend the afternoon settling in, then head out for a sunset Shikara ride — the classic first-evening Kashmir experience. Dinner on board with Wazwan cuisine caps off the day.

Day 2: Srinagar City & Mughal Gardens

Visit Nishat Bagh, Shalimar Bagh, and Chashme Shahi — Srinagar's three great Mughal-era gardens — followed by a walk through the old city and Lal Chowk market for handicraft shopping. Return to the houseboat by evening.

Day 3: Gulmarg Day Trip

Early departure (by 7-8 AM) for the 1.5-2 hour drive to Gulmarg. Ride the Gondola to Apharwat Peak, ski or play in the snow in winter, or enjoy meadow walks and golf in summer. See our full Gulmarg travel guide for details.

Day 4: Pahalgam Day Trip

Depart early for the 2.5-3 hour drive to Pahalgam, then take a local taxi to Betaab Valley, Aru Valley, and Chandanwari. This is the longest day-trip day, so an early start matters most here. Full details in our Pahalgam travel guide.

Day 5: Sonamarg Day Trip

Drive to Sonamarg (87 km, 2.5-3 hours) for Thajiwas Glacier and, if open, Zero Point near Zojila Pass. See our Sonamarg travel guide for seasonal notes — this is the most weather-dependent day of the trip.

Day 6: Dal Lake Deep Dive

A slower day back on the lake: a pre-dawn Shikara ride to the floating vegetable market, a cruise past the floating gardens and Char Chinar island, and an afternoon for pashmina and papier-mâché shopping. Read more in our Dal Lake guide.

Day 7: Departure

One last sunrise or sunset on the lake, breakfast on board, and a Shikara transfer to shore for your flight or train home.

Planning Tips

  • Start day trips early: 7-8 AM departures avoid both traffic and afternoon cloud cover at higher elevations
  • Confirm your houseboat handles logistics: A good owner-operated property can arrange all three day-trip taxis for you
  • Build in one buffer day if traveling in peak season (June-August), when Gondola queues and road traffic can slow things down
  • Check the season before finalizing the order: in deep winter, Sonamarg's road may be closed — see our best time to visit Kashmir guide

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 7 days enough for Kashmir?

Yes, 7 days is a comfortable amount of time to cover Srinagar and Dal Lake plus day trips to Gulmarg, Pahalgam, and Sonamarg without feeling rushed. It's the most commonly recommended trip length for a first visit to Kashmir.

How much does a 7-day Kashmir trip cost?

A 7-day Kashmir trip with a premium all-inclusive houseboat stay, day-trip taxis, and Gondola/entry tickets typically runs ₹35,000-70,000 per person depending on season and accommodation tier, excluding flights.

Should I stay in one place or move around for 7 days?

Basing yourself at a Dal Lake houseboat in Srinagar and doing Gulmarg, Pahalgam, and Sonamarg as day trips is generally more efficient than changing hotels each night, since all three are within a 1.5-3 hour drive of Srinagar.

Can this itinerary be extended to include Ladakh?

Yes — since Sonamarg sits on the Srinagar-Leh Highway, travelers with more time often extend this itinerary by continuing through Zojila Pass toward Leh, typically adding 4-7 more days.

What's the best order to visit Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg?

There's no strict requirement, but doing Gulmarg first (closest, most developed), Pahalgam second (moderate distance, needs the most time), and Sonamarg last (furthest, most weather-dependent) tends to work well logistically.
