Family Room

Spacious Houseboat Living for 3-6 Guests · Dal Lake, Srinagar

Room Type · Family

Room to Breathe, Together on Dal Lake

The Family Room is our most spacious accommodation — designed for families and small groups who want everyone under one roof without compromising on comfort. Multiple beds, generous storage, and wide lake-facing windows make it the natural choice for guests traveling with children, parents, or a group of friends exploring Kashmir together.

Quick Answer

The Family Room sleeps 3-6 guests across multiple beds, with full Dal Lake views, walnut wood interiors, and an attached modern bathroom. Rates typically run ₹10,000-13,000/night, with meal plans available. Book directly on WhatsApp for current availability.

Why Families Choose This Room

Unlike splitting a family across two smaller rooms, the Family Room keeps everyone together — useful for parents traveling with young children, or multi-generational groups who want to start and end each day as one unit. Owner Saif ul Rehman Guroo personally coordinates meal timing and Shikara rides around family schedules, including arranging life jackets for children on lake excursions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many guests can stay in the Family Room?

The Family Room comfortably accommodates 3-6 guests across multiple beds, making it well suited to families and small groups traveling together.

Does the Family Room have a Dal Lake view?

Yes, like every room at Olympias Athena, the Family Room has lake-facing windows with views of Dal Lake and the surrounding Zabarwan mountains.

Are meals included with the Family Room?

Both stay-with-meals and stay-without-meals packages are available. Authentic Kashmiri cuisine, including Wazwan, can be arranged for families with advance notice, and spice levels can be adjusted for children.
