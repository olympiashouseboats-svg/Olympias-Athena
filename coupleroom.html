Couple Room

Romantic Houseboat Suite for Two · Dal Lake, Srinagar

Room Type · Couple

A Quiet Corner of Dal Lake, Just for Two

The Couple Room is our most intimate accommodation — a king bed, silk drapes, and a private lakeside seating area set apart from the main sundeck. It's the room most guests choose for honeymoons and anniversaries, where a quiet evening watching the sun set over the Zabarwan mountains matters more than square footage.

Quick Answer

The Couple Room is a romantic suite for two, with a king bed, private lakeside seating, and hand-carved walnut interiors. Rates typically run ₹9,000-12,000/night, with private sunset Shikara rides available on request — a popular addition for honeymoons.

Why Couples Choose This Room

Most honeymoon and anniversary guests want privacy without losing the reason they came to Dal Lake in the first place — the water, the light, the mountains. The Couple Room's private seating area lets you take that in without sharing the moment with the rest of the boat, and owner Saif ul Rehman Guroo personally arranges small romantic touches — a private sunset Shikara, a quiet dinner setup — with advance notice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Couple Room good for a honeymoon?

Yes, the Couple Room is specifically designed for honeymoons and anniversaries, with romantic decor, a king bed, private lakeside seating, and options for a private sunset Shikara ride arranged by the owner.

Can I request a private Shikara ride with the Couple Room?

Yes, private sunset Shikara rides can be arranged in advance for couples staying in this room — a popular addition for honeymoons and anniversary celebrations.

Does the Couple Room have its own private seating area?

Yes, the Couple Room includes private lakeside seating separate from the main sundeck, giving couples a quieter space to enjoy Dal Lake views.
